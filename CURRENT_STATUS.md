through ownership write-back; this admission is additional to, not a
replacement for, cut feasibility checks (startup still applies no

(duplicate rejection plus full-coverage verification; `-1` cannot be a valid
MPI rank). Runtime rebalance and top-domain
seed admissions share `parallel::estimateRuntimeDecompositionSourceStorage`:
checked arithmetic with one gas-incidence condition, so DMO admits zero gas
estimate bytes, and seed admission adds caller-specific seed/leaf memory
instead of duplicated cell/patch terms.

capacity authority (`estimateParticleMemoryBytesForDecomposition` is the
source-view species table for storage admissions). New unit coverage (estimator-versus-storage consistency,

modes, error-path message identity, contract validation, explicit-component

and passes when compiled directly (g++ link + run, exit 0); the dense-index
guard and startup planner admission added by the P4 source-closure pass are
not yet covered — validation is deferred to P5. The standard
`cmake --preset cpu-only-debug` / `ctest --preset test-cpu-debug` floor was
blocked by environment permission rules this session; no production
qualification claim is made.

reported as global. Dependency-complete MPI/Parallel-HDF5/FFTW execution and
the real 64^3 three-rank first-light rerun remain qualification gates, not
claims made by this status entry.
